  <p class="opening-lede">You'll help build an experimental VR narrative where the story isn't just something you look at — it's something you move through, with six degrees of freedom shaping how the plot unfolds.</p>

  <h2>What you'll do</h2>
  <p>Prototype and test a real-time 6DoF VR narrative — one that sits somewhere between a game and a film — working alongside the lab's research team on storytelling techniques, spatial design, and interaction. If the work holds up, it goes further: toward a research paper on what actually makes 6DoF storytelling work. You'll be building on the same ground as <a href="/projects/Belonging/" target="_blank">Belonging</a> and <a href="/projects/manhole2_itm/" target="_blank">Into the Manhole</a>, two 6DoF narratives already made at IMXD Lab.</p>

  <h2>You'll be a good fit if</h2>
  <ul class="opening-fit-list">
    <li>You've got some Unity (C#) or Unreal (Blueprint) experience, or are eager to pick it up fast.</li>
    <li>You're interested in how VR, AR, or spatial design change what a "story" can be.</li>
    <li>You like the idea of research that ends in something people can actually put on and walk through — not just a paper.</li>
  </ul>

  <p class="opening-eligibility-note">Open to 3rd/4th-year Bachelor's or 2nd-year Master's students in Computer Science, Multimedia and Gaming, Animation &amp; VFX, Design or related fields.</p>
