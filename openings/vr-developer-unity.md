<p class="opening-lede">This internship involves working on an ongoing VR project at the lab, developing and refining the experience in Unity into a build that runs well on VR headsets.</p>

<h2>Role Overview</h2>
<p>You'll join the project team in person, developing interactions, shaping levels, and testing the VR experience in Unity, working alongside the lab's team on interaction and spatial design.</p>
<p>You'll work from the IMXD Lab, with access to its <a href="{{ '/about#facilities' | relative_url }}">VR, mixed reality and motion capture facilities</a>.</p>

<h2>Eligibility</h2>
<p>Open to 3rd and 4th year Bachelor's students and 2nd year Master's students.</p>
<p>Open to, but not limited to, students from Design, Animation, Gaming, Interaction Design, and Computer Science with a focus on Game Design or Development.</p>

<h2>Desired Qualifications</h2>
<ul class="opening-fit-list">
  <li>Proficiency in C# scripting.</li>
  <li>Well versed with the Unity game engine ecosystem, including VR development tools such as the XR Interaction Toolkit or Meta XR SDK.</li>
  <li>Experience developing interactions in Unity, along with greyboxing and level design.</li>
  <li>Good understanding of building and deploying VR projects, both as an APK for standalone headsets such as Meta Quest and as an EXE for PC VR.</li></ul>
