modaltext: "Heads the IMXD Lab and teaches in the Interaction Design discipline at IDC School of Design. An academician, designer and filmmaker, his research focuses on storytelling and presence in virtual reality, including the \"Grammar of VR Storytelling\". He holds a PhD in Virtual Reality from Arts et Métiers ParisTech, France, an M.Des from IIT Kanpur, and a B.Arch from Kerala University. He works on <a href=\"/projects/foah\">Fragments of a Home</a>, and co-directs the <a href=\"/manhole-project\">Manhole project</a>, which includes the award-winning VR narrative <a href=\"/projects/into-the-manhole\">Into the Manhole</a> and the short film <a href=\"/projects/manhole-short-film\">Manhole</a> (2022). He has also led lab projects including <a href=\"/projects/Belonging\">Belonging</a>, <a href=\"/projects/cinevoque\">Cinévoqué</a> and <a href=\"/projects/dragonfly\">Dragonfly</a>."
