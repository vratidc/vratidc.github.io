Khyati Priya, a PhD student at the IDC School of Design, IIT Bombay, successfully defended her doctoral thesis, *Communicating Change through Form: Studying Perceived Affordances in 4D Printed Artefacts*, on 17 November 2026.

Her research was supervised by Prof. Jayesh Pillai and Prof. Avinash Shende. Congratulations, Khyati!
