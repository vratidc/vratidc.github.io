Players secretly choose and reveal their cards, scan them using Vuforia, and battle based on their monster stats. The winner of each round earns a point, with the highest score winning after five rounds.</br></br>Built in Unity for Android using Vuforia for image tracking. Cards act as image targets that summon 3D monsters, while Unity scripts handle stats, battle logic, scoring, rounds, and UI. Animations, VFX, lighting, and sound effects enhance the AR battle experience.</br></br>"
