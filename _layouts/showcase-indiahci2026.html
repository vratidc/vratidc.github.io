A Celebration – a VR experience of a painting

VR Unreal Engine Installation

This is an inter-media interactive art experience. In this exhibit, the audience get to view an original painting by the artist Nandhini Sreekumar, followed by a chance to step inside the painting and experience it spatially in a 6DoF Virtual Reality environment, with interactions and a narrative. The experience was created in Unreal engine, applying novel techniques of character scanning, stroke based texturing by the artist, and the concept of 2D art being a spatial interactive environment.
