## Collaborate with us

Alongside student and research work, the lab takes up sponsored research and consultancy projects that align with its research areas, in collaboration with industry, government and other institutions. Recent collaborations include the Drone Ecosystem Hub, Nanavati Hospital and ShipTech-ICON. These projects are taken up through the [Industrial Research and Consultancy Centre (IRCC)](https://rnd.iitb.ac.in/){:target="_blank"} of the Dean (R&D) office, IIT Bombay.

For sponsored research, consultancy or other collaborations, [write to us](mailto:hello@imxd.in?cc=jay@iitb.ac.in&subject=Collaboration%20Enquiry%20-%20IMXD%20Lab){:target="_blank"}.
